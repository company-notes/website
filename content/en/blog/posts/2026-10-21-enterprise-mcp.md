# Connect Bots to real company systems

A curated list of tools and patterns for running an AI-operated organization. Not every component belongs in every company.

<!-- more -->

![Connect Bots to real company systems](../../assets/covers/integrations.svg)

| Tool | Why it might help |
| --- | --- |
| [FastFileLink / FFL MCP](https://github.com/nuwainfo/ffl-mcp) | Deliver large artifacts to outside recipients |
| [MailTrigger](https://www.mailtrigger.net/) | Route and automate incoming email events |
| [Wekan](https://wekan.github.io/) | Task boards and collaboration |
| [Memos](https://www.usememos.com/) | Notes and operating memory |
| [ERPNext](https://erpnext.com/) | Business operations and approvals |

Open scripts, examples and MCP connectors can be linked here when they are published.
